# Why Do we require BASH Only?

Yes, I know dash and ash are "smaller" shells
but I do not care 90% of what I do is either 
linux or it is Git-BASH on windows. It is not DASH.

I have been using BASH for 30 years now. Its wired
into my brain in many ways.

So why fight that? I do not see a meaningful difference
between BASH vrs DASH - anything less than 0.5 second is not
meaningful in my view.

We all have gigs of ram, and it is a script and BASH has so many 
more wonderful features that simplify things for me

I do not have the time or energy to go change my brain
and debug problems like "Oh crap, DASH does not support that"

I will not support that.
